### How to regenerate training evidence (if needed for the deck)
`finetune.py` prints per-step loss via a `PrintLossCallback` (`Step N | loss=…`). To capture a loss trace:
1. Run the Colab path: `python finetune.py` locally emits `finetune_colab.ipynb` when MPS training fails.
2. Open that notebook in Google Colab with a **T4 GPU**, upload `train.jsonl`, run all cells.
3. Screenshot the training output cell (the streaming `Step N | loss=…` lines) — that is your loss-curve evidence.
